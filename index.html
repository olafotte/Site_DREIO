Specialized Engineering Services

Submarine Cable Engineering & EPCI

Independent drafting and review of technical specifications for offshore EPCI tenders and contracts.

  • Independent engineering statements for in-service cables.
  • Field survey data review (ROV video footage, MBES, and monitoring data).

Advanced Multiphysics Modeling & FEA

Advanced numerical modeling to determine thermal, electrical, and mechanical operational limits of subsea cables and accessories.

  • Hydrodynamic, static, and dynamic global analysis in OrcaFlex.
  • Coupled electromagnetic and thermal modeling in COMSOL (CIGRE TB 880).
  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA) in Ansys and Abaqus for umbilicals and accessories.

R&D, Qualification & Floating Wind

Research, development, and strategic advisory for dynamic high-voltage applications in deep and ultra-deep waters.

  • Preliminary technical feasibility studies (Desktop Studies) for floating wind.
  • Supplier technical qualification and factory audit processes.
  • Failure root-cause analysis and laboratory test specification.
